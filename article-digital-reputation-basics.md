  <section itemprop="articleBody">
    <h2>Why Your Digital Reputation Matters More Than Ever</h2>
    <p>In today's hyper-connected world, your digital reputation isn't just important—it's everything. Before anyone does business with you, hires you, or even meets you, they search for you online. What they find in those first few search results determines their perception of you.</p>

    <div class="statistics">
      <h3>The Statistics Don't Lie:</h3>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>75% of recruiters</strong> research candidates online</li>
        <li><strong>85% of consumers</strong> trust online reviews as much as personal recommendations</li>
        <li><strong>60% of businesses</strong> have experienced reputation damage from online content</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2>The Three Pillars of Digital Reputation</h2>
    
    <div class="pillar">
      <h3>1. Visibility Management</h3>
      <p>Controlling what appears when people search for you or your business. This includes:</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Search engine results</li>
        <li>Social media profiles</li>
        <li>Online directory listings</li>
        <li>Review platform presence</li>
      </ul>
    </div>

    <div class="pillar">
      <h3>2. Content Strategy</h3>
      <p>Creating and promoting positive, authentic content that represents your values and expertise:</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Professional blog content</li>
        <li>Social media updates</li>
        <li>Industry contributions</li>
        <li>Personal branding content</li>
      </ul>
    </div>

    <div class="pillar">
      <h3>3. Risk Mitigation</h3>
      <p>Proactively identifying and addressing potential reputation threats:</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Regular monitoring and alerts</li>
        <li>Crisis response planning</li>
        <li>Negative content management</li>
        <li>Legal protection strategies</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2>Essential Tools for Getting Started</h2>
    
    <div class="tools-section">
      <h3>Free Tools Every Business Should Use:</h3>
      <ol>
        <li><strong>Google Alerts</strong> - Monitor mentions of your name and brand</li>
        <li><strong>Google Search Console</strong> - Track your search performance</li>
        <li><strong>Social Searcher</strong> - Social media monitoring across platforms</li>
      </ol>
    </div>

    <div class="tools-section">
      <h3>When to Consider Premium Tools:</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>When your business scales beyond local reach</li>
        <li>When you need real-time alerts</li>
        <li>When dealing with reputation crises</li>
        <li>When you need comprehensive analytics</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2>Common Mistakes to Avoid</h2>
    
    <div class="mistake" itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/HowToTip">
      <h3>❌ Mistake 1: The "Set It and Forget It" Approach</h3>
      <p itemprop="text">Many businesses create social profiles and then abandon them. Inactive profiles can damage your reputation more than having no profile at all.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="mistake" itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/HowToTip">
      <h3>❌ Mistake 2: Ignoring Negative Content</h3>
      <p itemprop="text">Hope is not a strategy. Negative content rarely disappears on its own—it needs active management.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="mistake" itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/HowToTip">
      <h3>❌ Mistake 3: Inconsistent Branding</h3>
      <p itemprop="text">Different names, photos, or information across platforms confuses potential customers and damages trust.</p>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2>Your Action Plan for the Next 30 Days</h2>
    
    <div class="timeline">
      <div class="week">
        <h3>Week 1: Audit & Assessment</h3>
        <ul>
          <li>Google yourself and your business</li>
          <li>Audit all social media profiles</li>
          <li>Set up basic monitoring alerts</li>
        </ul>
      </div>

      <div class="week">
        <h3>Week 2-3: Content Foundation</h3>
        <ul>
          <li>Claim all relevant social profiles</li>
          <li>Create consistent bios and descriptions</li>
          <li>Publish your first professional content</li>
        </ul>
      </div>

      <div class="week">
        <h3>Week 4: Ongoing Management</h3>
        <ul>
          <li>Establish weekly monitoring routine</li>
          <li>Plan monthly content calendar</li>
          <li>Set up crisis response protocols</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>
